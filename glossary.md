# Glossary — Terms used in this vault

Hover a wikilink in Obsidian for a preview; click to read the full note. Each term also links back to the foundation notes where it's introduced.

## Index (alphabetical)

- [Band plan](glossary/band-plan.md) — voluntary agreement about what frequencies within a band are used for what
- [Callsign](glossary/callsign.md) — unique FCC-issued operator identifier (ham, GMRS, commercial)
- [Channel](glossary/channel.md) — one pre-programmed radio config: RX, TX, mode, tones/color code, power, etc.
- [Codeplug](glossary/codeplug.md) — complete bundle of a radio's configuration, written to the radio as a file
- [Color code](glossary/color-code.md) — DMR's access-control number (0–15), equivalent of CTCSS on analog
- [CTCSS](glossary/ctcss.md) — sub-audible tone (analog FM) for squelch access / repeater access
- [DCS](glossary/dcs.md) — digital-coded squelch, DCS codes as an alternative to CTCSS
- [Duplex](glossary/duplex.md) — TX and RX on different frequencies, as used with repeaters
- [Part 90 / Part 95](glossary/part-90-95.md) — FCC sections governing commercial vs GMRS equipment certification
- [Repeater](glossary/repeater.md) — tower-mounted station that listens on one freq and retransmits on another
- [Simplex](glossary/simplex.md) — TX and RX on the same frequency; no repeater
- [Talkgroup](glossary/talkgroup.md) — numeric label tagging a DMR conversation for audience filtering
- [Timeslot](glossary/timeslot.md) — DMR Tier II's two-conversations-per-frequency TDMA slots (TS1, TS2)
- [Tone squelch](glossary/tone-squelch.md) — umbrella term for CTCSS/DCS mute-unless-matching-tone mechanisms
- [Zone](glossary/zone.md) — named group of channels selectable as a unit on the radio
