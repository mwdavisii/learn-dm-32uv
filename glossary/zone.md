# Zone

A named group of [channel](channel.md)s selectable as a unit on the radio. On an HT, the zone selector is the first thing you turn to after powering on: pick a zone ("Memphis 2 m," "GMRS," "Weather"), then spin the channel knob within that zone.

Zones exist because flat lists of 200+ channels are unusable on a tiny screen. The DM-32UV supports dozens of zones with tens of channels each. Good zone design keeps each zone focused (one band or one purpose) and small enough that the channel knob doesn't require ten revolutions to find anything.

In this codeplug: Zone 1 GMRS, Zone 2 Memphis 2 m, Zone 3 Memphis 70 cm, Zone 4 Memphis DMR, Zone 5 Weather, Zone 6 Monitor.
