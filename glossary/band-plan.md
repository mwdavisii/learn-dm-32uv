# Band plan

A **voluntary agreement** among operators about which frequencies within a band are used for what. Not FCC rule (mostly); rather, community consensus published by organizations like the ARRL.

Example — 2 m band plan excerpts (US, ARRL):

- 144.000–144.100 MHz — CW only
- 144.200 MHz — SSB calling frequency
- 144.390 MHz — APRS
- 146.400–146.580 MHz — FM simplex
- **146.520 MHz — national 2 m FM calling frequency**
- 146.600–147.400 MHz — repeater inputs/outputs (with 600 kHz offset)
- 146.000–148.000 MHz — FM repeaters

Band plans exist because a 4 MHz-wide band shared by thousands of operators would be chaos without convention. Following the band plan keeps you off toes — CW operators don't want FM in the CW section, digital modes cluster in assigned sub-bands, etc.

Your programmed simplex and repeater channels respect the Memphis-area 2 m and 70 cm band plans. See [Simplex calling](../05-memphis-setup/simplex-calling.md) when populated.
