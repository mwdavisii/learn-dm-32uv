# Simplex

Two-way radio where everyone uses the **same frequency** to transmit and receive, taking turns. Only one person can talk at a time; everyone else listens.

Examples: the national 2 m calling frequency **146.520 MHz** is simplex. Two hikers calling each other on GMRS channel 1 are simplex. CB channel 19 is simplex.

Contrast with [duplex](duplex.md) (repeater operation), where TX and RX are on different frequencies.

Range on simplex equals your actual line-of-sight plus a bit of bending around obstacles — no assistance from a tall tower. On a 5 W HT in an urban environment, that's typically 1–5 miles.

See: [duplex](duplex.md), [repeater](repeater.md), [channel](channel.md)
