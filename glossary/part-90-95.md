# Part 90 / Part 95

Sections of **47 CFR** (the FCC rulebook) that govern different radio services, each with its own equipment type-acceptance requirements.

- **Part 90** — private land mobile service: commercial/business/public-safety two-way radio. Also the home for most ham-world DMR radios, which are typically certified under both Part 90 and marked as ham-capable.
- **Part 95 Subpart E** (Part 95E) — GMRS. Requires specifically Part-95E-certified equipment.
- **Part 97** — Amateur. Mostly doesn't require type-acceptance; hams can homebrew.

Your **DM-32UV is Part 90** (plus ham-capable), **not Part 95E**. This means transmitting on GMRS with it is technically non-compliant even though you hold a valid GMRS license.

Enforcement on personal/family GMRS use with Part-90 gear is effectively zero, and you've chosen to proceed with that tradeoff. Full context in [the foundations note](../01-foundations/part90-vs-part95.md).
